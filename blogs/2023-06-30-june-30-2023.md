v1 Feature Three functions are available: map.merge takes two maps, creates a copy of the first map, and adds items from the second map to the copy; map.merge_nested recursively adds items from a map to a copy of another map; uuid.generate returns a random universally unique identifier. Feature Support for invoking a VPC Service Controls-compliant private endpoint is available in Preview .
